How Your Bonus Works

💰 Your Bonus Structure

Your bonus is 0.3% of Skin Social's total monthly revenue. This is an upgrade from your previous flat $200 cap — now it scales with the practice. At $100K = $300 max. At $125K = $375 max. 80% comes from the 4 team goals. 20% comes from your personal lead response time.

⚡ Speed Is Money

Every lead that goes unanswered for more than 5 minutes has a 50%+ lower chance of booking. You are often the first impression a new patient gets of Skin Social. Fast, warm responses convert strangers into patients. This is one of the highest-ROI behaviors in the entire practice.

📅 Payment Schedule

Calculated on the 1st of the following month. Paid with the second paycheck of that month.
