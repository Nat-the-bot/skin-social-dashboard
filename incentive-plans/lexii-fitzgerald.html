How Your Bonus Works

💰 How Your Bonus Is Calculated

Your bonus is 0.5% of Skin Social's total monthly revenue. As the practice grows, your bonus grows automatically — no renegotiation needed. At $100K practice revenue = $500 max. At $125K = $625 max. Your score (1–5) on your 5 goals determines what % of that you receive.

📈 Your Earning Potential

$100K practice month: Max = $500  ·  $115K month: Max = $575  ·  $125K month: Max = $625. The better Skin Social does, the better you do — you're invested in the whole practice.

📅 When You're Paid

Bonus calculated using practice revenue report on the 1st of the following month. Paid with the second paycheck. Natalie reviews your scorecard with you in your monthly 1:1.
