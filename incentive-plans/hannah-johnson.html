How Your Bonus Works

💰 How Your Bonus Is Calculated

Your bonus is 4% of your personal monthly revenue — the revenue you personally generate from your patient appointments. No fixed ceiling: the more you produce, the more you earn. Minimum threshold: $20,000 personal revenue required to be eligible. Example: You generate $28,000 → bonus pool = $1,120. Your score (1–5) then determines what % of that pool you receive.

📈 Your Earning Potential

$25,000/mo: Pool = $1,000  ·  $30,000/mo: Pool = $1,200  ·  $35,000/mo: Pool = $1,400  ·  $40,000/mo: Pool = $1,600. Score 5.0 on all goals = 100% of pool. This plan has no ceiling — it grows with you.

📅 When You're Paid

Bonus calculated on the 1st of the following month using your Boulevard revenue report. Paid with the second paycheck of that month. Natalie reviews your scorecard with you in your monthly 1:1.
