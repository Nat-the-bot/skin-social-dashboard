How Your Bonus Works

💰 How Your Bonus Is Calculated

Your bonus is 0.3% of Skin Social's total monthly revenue. This upgrades your previous flat $200/month plan — now your bonus scales with the practice. At $100K = $300 max. At $125K = $375 max. Your score on 5 goals determines what % you receive.

📈 This Is an Upgrade

Your previous plan was capped at $200/month flat. This plan starts at $300 and grows automatically as the practice grows. No awkward renegotiation conversations — it scales with you.

📅 When You're Paid

Bonus calculated on the 1st of the following month. Paid with the second paycheck of that month.
