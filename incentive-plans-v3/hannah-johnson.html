This document outlines your individual incentive plan for 2026. It is designed to be straightforward: there are four team goals that the entire Skin Social team works toward together, and one personal goal that reflects your specific role and contribution.

Your bonus has two components. Eighty percent comes from the team goals — meaning your bonus is directly tied to how well the practice performs as a whole. Twenty percent comes from your personal goal — the one area where your individual effort has the most direct impact. Both are measured monthly and paid together.

This plan is built to grow with you. As Skin Social grows, the bonus pool grows. As your performance improves, your payout increases. There are no tricks and no fine print — just clear targets and a direct path to earning more.

These four goals belong to the entire team. Every person at Skin Social contributes to each one. When the team hits these targets, every team member receives the same payout — regardless of role. The team wins together.

Goal 2  —  Patient Retention
Team Goal
Patient retention measures how many of our patients return for follow-up appointments. It is the single most important indicator of a healthy practice. Retaining an existing patient costs far less than acquiring a new one — and retained patients spend more, refer more, and leave more reviews. We track retention at three intervals to see the full picture: how many patients come back within 30 days, 60 days, and 90 days of their last visit.
Window What It Measures Where We Are Now Our Target Industry Standard
30-Day Patients who rebook within 30 days of their visit 25.8% 30% 40%
60-Day Patients who rebook within 60 days of their visit 34.0% 40% 50%
90-Day Patients who rebook within 90 days of their visit 37.0% 45% 55%

To earn this goal, the team must hit at least two of the three targets in a given month. Hitting all three earns the maximum retention score.

Scoring Rubric
Score What It Means
1 The team meets none of the three retention targets (30-day below 30%, 60-day below 40%, 90-day below 45%).
2 The team meets one of the three retention targets.
3 The team meets two of the three retention targets. This is the minimum to earn the retention portion of the bonus.
4 The team meets all three retention targets (30-day 30%+, 60-day 40%+, 90-day 45%+).
5 The team meets all three targets AND at least one window reaches industry standard (30-day 40%+, 60-day 50%+, or 90-day 55%+).
How it is tracked: Boulevard rebooking report, pulled on the 1st of the following month. Measured practice-wide.
