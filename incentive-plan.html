How It Works — The Simple Version
Read this first. Everything else builds on this.

One Bonus. Four Goals. Paid Every Month.

Every month, Skin Social sets a bonus pool based on how much revenue we bring in. The bigger the month, the bigger the pool.

But here's the key: the pool is split into 4 slices. Each slice is only paid out if the team hits the matching goal that month. If we crush revenue but forget about Google reviews — we lose that slice. Every goal matters. Every person plays a role.

On top of the team pool, providers and the esthetician earn individual bonuses based on their personal rebooking rate, and the social media manager earns a bonus for new patient growth.

1

💰 Revenue determines the prize pool

The higher our monthly revenue, the bigger the pool everyone splits. Hit $100K = $1,500 pool. Hit $125K = $3,500 pool. This keeps everyone invested in the big number.

2

🔑 4 goals unlock 4 slices of the pool

The pool is divided into 4 buckets. Each bucket only opens if that goal is met. Miss a goal = that money stays locked. Hit all 4 = everyone gets the full pool.

3

👤 Individual bonuses reward personal performance

Providers and the esthetician earn extra based on their own rebooking rate. The social media manager earns extra based on new patient count. These are personal — you control them.

4

📊 We review the scorecard on the 1st of every month

One simple scorecard. Did we hit our goals? Bonuses are calculated and paid within the first week of the following month. No guessing, no drama — just numbers.

Common Questions
What happens if we have a slow month below $90K?
No team pool that month — but individual bonuses (rebooking + new patients) are still earnable. This protects the business during slower periods while still rewarding personal effort.
What happened to the weekly Google review prize?
It's been replaced by this plan. Instead of small weekly prizes that cost money regardless of how the business is doing, you now earn a much larger monthly amount tied to real business results. The weekly goal of 10/week still stands — it just feeds into the monthly 40/month target.
When do we get paid?
Bonuses are calculated on the 1st of the following month and paid with the second paycheck of that month. So April's bonus is paid in early May.
How do I know my personal rebooking rate?
Boulevard tracks this per provider. You can ask to see your report any time. Natalie will share everyone's individual rate at the monthly team review — transparently, so everyone knows where they stand and can improve.
Can this plan change?
Yes — we'll review it quarterly. If goals become too easy or too hard, we'll adjust together. The goal is for this to feel fair and motivating, not frustrating.
Is this in addition to my regular pay?
Yes. This is 100% additional compensation on top of your base salary or hourly rate. It does not replace any existing pay.
