Financial Pain Points
Where to focus attention

💊 COGS Volatility — Your Biggest Wild Card

COGS swings between 10% and 65% of revenue month to month — almost entirely due to uneven injectable purchasing (bulk prepayments to McKesson, Galderma, Merz). This is cash-flow dangerous and distorts your true profitability picture. Solution: establish a monthly COGS budget of ~$20-25K and stick to it regardless of vendor deals.

👩‍⚕️ Payroll — Scaling Faster Than Revenue

Payroll grew from $5,725/mo (Sep 2024) to $31,097/mo (Mar 2026) — a 5x increase vs 3x revenue growth. Jan 2026 hit a scary 42.9% of revenue on payroll alone. Target: payroll should stay at or below 28-30% of revenue. You need a staffing model tied to revenue thresholds.

📆 Seasonality — Summer & Year-Start Dips

Clear dips in Jul 2025 ($66K) and Jan 2026 ($76K). Summer and post-holiday slowdowns are real. You need a slow-month revenue strategy — membership packages, prepaid treatment bundles, or targeted promotions to smooth the curve.

🔧 Equipment Rental — Fixed Drag

Dext Capital equipment rental runs $2,827–$2,876/mo every single month — $33,932/year. This is a fixed cost that doesn't scale. At current revenue levels it's only 2.6% of revenue, but at $40K revenue months it was 7%. Evaluate whether buying vs. renting makes sense now that you're at scale.

📉 Loss Months — Misleading but Real

5 of 19 months showed negative net income: Oct 2024, Feb 2025, Aug 2025, Dec 2025, Jan 2026. Dec 2025 and Jan 2026 losses are largely distortions (non-cash depreciation + bulk COGS prepayments), but Oct 2024 and Feb 2025 represent real operational losses requiring attention.

🏢 Rent Irregularities

Rent fluctuates — Feb 2025 shows $13,033 (double payment?), Jun 2025 shows $8,656, Nov 2025 shows $8,344. Normal rate appears to be $6,516–$6,998. These anomalies should be reconciled with your accountant — double payments or catch-ups cost real money.

CFO Strategic Recommendations
High-impact actions for the next 12 months

1️⃣ Normalize Your COGS — Build a Purchasing Calendar

Stop buying injectables in unpredictable spikes. Set a monthly COGS budget of $22–26K and spread purchases evenly. This will smooth your P&L dramatically and make your financials look better to any future investor or bank.

2️⃣ Revenue-Linked Staffing Model

Define revenue triggers for new hires. Example: "We add a provider when we sustain $100K+/mo for 2+ consecutive months." This prevents the Aug 2025 / Jan 2026 payroll runaway from recurring.

3️⃣ Launch a Membership Program

Your seasonality data shows predictable summer and January dips. A monthly membership ($99–$199/mo) for skincare or maintenance treatments creates guaranteed recurring revenue that cushions those valleys.

4️⃣ Equipment — Buy vs Lease Analysis

You're paying ~$34K/year to rent equipment. At 3 years, that's $100K+ with no asset to show. Get a quote to purchase your equipment outright — at your cash position ($62K+), a financed purchase may be smarter than perpetual rental.

5️⃣ Build a 3-Month Operating Reserve

Your monthly fixed costs are ~$45–55K. You should have $135–165K in a dedicated reserve account — untouchable except for emergencies. Your current $62K cash is good but not yet there. Prioritize this in 2026.

6️⃣ Product Sales — Underperforming

Product revenue averages only 3–5% of total ($3–5K/mo). For a med spa with premium skincare, this should be 10–15%. There's $7–12K/month of unrealized revenue sitting in your retail wall. Train staff on upselling protocols.
